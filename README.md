# Blast Bros

A twin-stick co-op shooter in the spirit of the old mobile classic Gun Bros. It's an unofficial fan homage with its own names, art and music, and it isn't affiliated with any other game or company.

**Play:** https://albertshi31.github.io/blast-bros/

- Desktop: WASD moves, the mouse aims, hold click to fire. Q swaps guns, 1 uses a syringe, 2 throws a grenade, R triggers Bro Rage.
- Phone: the left thumb moves and the right thumb aims and fires.

Progress saves in your browser.
